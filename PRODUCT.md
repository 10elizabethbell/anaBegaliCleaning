# Product: Ana Begali Cleaning Services website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page business site)

## Users
- Primary: people in Philadelphia and the surrounding area who need a home, office, Airbnb or job site cleaned. Most arrive from a Facebook post on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser. (inferred from where the business advertises)
- Secondary: the business itself (Ana Begali), who receives the site for free and should recognise her business in it within one screen.

## Purpose
Turn a phone number in a Facebook group post into a real home for the business: say what they clean, where, and how they treat a client's home, then get the visitor to call or text for a quote.

## Positioning
Residential and commercial cleaning in Philadelphia and surrounding areas, committed to "quality, organization, and punctuality" (their words).

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact is by phone: (267) 255-7148. Whether it takes texts is unknown, so calling stays visible (header) next to the text-based quote.
- No email, no booking link, no hours, no prices, no reviews, no photos of work, no logo (brief, 2026-10-18).
- No business Facebook page; the ad was posted from a personal profile.

## Brand commitments
- Name: "Ana Begali Cleaning Services" as written in the ad.
- Voice: warm, "we", emoji-forward on social ("Your home deserves special care! 🧼✨", "Focus on your day — we'll take care of the cleaning! 💚"). The site keeps the warmth without emoji.
- Colors: none known; green taken from their 💚 (inferred).
- Do not claim Ana Begali is the owner (unconfirmed).

## Evidence on hand
- Verbatim service list: regular and deep cleaning; commercial and office cleaning; Airbnb and move-in/move-out cleaning; post-construction cleaning; sofa and upholstery cleaning.
- Promise: "We are committed to quality, organization, and punctuality!"
- Area: "Philadelphia and surrounding areas".

## Principles
- Phone first. Facts only from the brief. No invented reviews, prices, years or photos.
- Speak as "we", about how they treat the client's home.
